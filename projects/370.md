# Legged Walking Robot
Fusion 360 | 3D Printing | Laser Cutting

## Problem
Students often do not have time between classes to eat. This project explores a robotic solution to this question.

## Solution
We created a four-legged robot that would walk around campus and dispense a piece of candy to students at targeted intervals of about 2 meters.
