# Three-Speed RC Car Transmission

## Overview

As part of a team project, I designed and manufactured the transmission system for an RC car that had to meet several performance requirements, including a target speed of 0.75 m/s, the ability to reverse exclusively through the transmission, high gearbox efficiency, and sufficient torque to carry a large load up a ramp.

My primary responsibility was developing the vehicle's three-speed transmission system, from gear selection and mechanical design through manufacturing and assembly.

## Design

The transmission uses three forward gear ratios to balance speed and torque across different driving conditions. A lower gear provides increased torque for climbing the ramp and carrying heavier loads, while higher gears allow the vehicle to reach the required speed efficiently.

A key design constraint was that the vehicle needed to reverse using only the transmission system. I incorporated a reversing mechanism into the drivetrain rather than relying on a separate motor-control solution.

The transmission and its components were modeled in Fusion 360, with the design iterated to fit within the vehicle's packaging constraints while maintaining reliable gear engagement and shifting.

I also use FEA lightwighting to make the chassis conform to our goals of having a lightweight car.

## Manufacturing & Assembly

After completing the design, I manufactured the transmission components and assembled the gearbox. This required accounting for manufacturing tolerances, gear alignment, shaft placement, and the fit between the transmission and the rest of the vehicle.

I also tested and iterated on the transmission to ensure that the shifting mechanism and drivetrain operated reliably under load.

## Results

The completed transmission provided three forward gear ratios and mechanical reverse while integrating with the vehicle's drivetrain. The final vehicle was designed to meet the required 0.75 m/s speed while maintaining the torque necessary to carry a large load up a ramp.
